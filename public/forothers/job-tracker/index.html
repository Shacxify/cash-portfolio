==== PHASE 1: INTERVIEW ME (one question at a time) ====
Start by saying hi in two sentences and asking the first question. Collect all of these, in this order, adapting the wording to what I've already told you:
1. My first name, and what I'd like you to call me.
2. The kind of work I'm looking for. Ask me to describe it the way I'd tell a friend, not with job titles. Then suggest 6 to 10 job titles that match what I said and ask me to keep or cut each one. Group them into Tier 1 (ideal, highest pay), Tier 2 (good), and Tier 3 (acceptable backup). Ask about titles I never want to see.
3. My experience level: years in that kind of work, highest education finished or in progress, and anything I'm certified or licensed in.
4. Where I'll work: city or metro areas, how far I'd commute, and whether remote, hybrid, or in-person is fine. Ask whether I'd relocate.
5. The lowest yearly pay (or hourly rate) I'd take. If I don't know, help me think about it with a range for my field and area, then let me pick.
6. Deal-breakers: industries I won't work in, schedules I can't do (nights, weekends, travel), company types to avoid (staffing agencies, commission-only, door-to-door), and anything else.
7. Things that would make a posting a great fit (certain companies, benefits, union, bilingual roles, a mission I care about).
8. How often I want it to run. Default is weekdays at 9:00am and 4:00pm my time. Confirm my time zone.
9. The Gmail address I use when I apply to jobs (so the task can check for replies).
10. Whether I want a phone notification only when something is urgent (a deadline within 48 hours, an interview request) or every time it runs.

After the interview, read back a short summary of everything in a clean list and ask me to confirm or fix anything. Do not continue until I say it's right.
